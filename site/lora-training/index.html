LoRA training

Train a FLUX LoRA on your own avatar

Turn any Studio avatar into a FLUX LoRA, then reuse that likeness in every later render. Training runs on your own fal.ai account at $2 per 1,000 steps, with the total shown before you start.

Quick startView on GitHub

What LoRA training costs

Length Steps Cost on fal.ai
Fast 500 $1.00
Balanced 1,000 $2.00
Best 2,000 $4.00

Renders with your trained model use FLUX.1 LoRA on fal.ai at $0.035 per megapixel, about $0.035 for a one-megapixel image. SunnyGen adds no markup; charges go to your own fal account.

How training works

Step 1

Choose the training images

Pick an avatar you already keep in Studio and select 10–40 crops cut from its character sheet and 4×4 panel.

Step 2

Pick a length and trigger word

Fast, Balanced, or Best sets the step count. The trigger word is added to the front of every prompt that uses the model.

Step 3

Review and start

The review lists the image count, training length, trigger word, and total cost. Confirm you have permission to train on the person's likeness, then start training.

Track runs and render with your model

The Studio Models tab lists every run with its status, steps, cost, and training date. A failed run keeps its reason and a retry button.

A trained model shows up as FLUX.1 LoRA in the home prompt bar, with a strength slider and its per-megapixel price. Attach the avatar as a reference and generate.
